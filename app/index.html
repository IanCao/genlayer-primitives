Your bond is returned if nobody challenges within the window.

How it settles

  1. OPEN - bonded, challenge window running. No model, no cost.
  2. CHALLENGED - someone matched the bond and disputed it.
  3. RESOLVED - validators decided. Nothing is paid yet.
  4. APPEALED - the loser matched the bond and filed counter evidence. The second reading cannot see the first.
  5. SETTLED - paid, final. Winner takes the pot.

On a contract without an appeal round the flow stops at RESOLVED-less settlement: the verdict pays immediately.
